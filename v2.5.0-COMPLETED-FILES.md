## 📊 Feature Summary

### What's New in v2.5.0:

✅ **Flexible Field Names**
- "Additional Cost Field 1/2/3" instead of restrictive "Pearl/Stone/Extra"
- Can be used for ANY purpose (Gemstone, Packaging, Certification, Labor, etc.)

✅ **Custom Labels Everywhere**
- Product editor shows your custom labels
- Frontend price breakup shows your custom labels
- Detailed breakup shows your custom labels
- No confusion about what each field represents

✅ **Percentage Calculations**
- Just like Making/Wastage charges
- Can be Fixed amount or % of metal price
- Automatically calculated during price updates

✅ **Visual Indicators**
- Shows **(₹)** for fixed amounts
- Shows **(% of metal price)** for percentages
- Dynamic help text guides users

✅ **Backward Compatible**
- Existing products work unchanged
- Default labels if not customized
- No database migration needed
- No data loss
